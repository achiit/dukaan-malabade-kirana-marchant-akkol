Our story

A counter our family has kept for 70 years

My grandfather started this shop on Main Peth Line about seventy years ago with a few sacks and a hand scale. I still remember sitting on a gunny bag beside him as a boy, learning to weigh dal without letting the pan tip even a little.

My father carried the shop forward and now I stand at the same counter. Many of our customers today are the grandchildren of the people who first bought from my grandfather. That is why we cannot afford to cheat anyone, not even by a few grams.
